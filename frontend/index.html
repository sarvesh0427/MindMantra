About This Project

MindMantra: A Gentle Mental Health Companion

This project is a final year capstone project developed by a Computer Engineering student from the School of Engineering, Pokhara University. It's an AI-powered support tool designed to help people gently understand and navigate mental health challenges.

What it offers:

  • Symptom-based reflection to help surface possible patterns.
  • Actionable, caring precautions to support your recovery journey.

The goal of this platform is simple: make mental wellbeing feel a little more approachable, and encourage people to talk about how they're really doing — without fear of judgment.
